📝 四、四级通用写作模板（5 大类型）

模板 1
利弊分析型（最常用，占总题量约 40%）
适用：科技话题、设施开放讨论、AI 工具使用等
【首段 — 引入话题】
In recent years, the issue of ______ has aroused wide public concern.
People hold different opinions on this matter.

【中段 — 分析利弊】
On the one hand, there are some advantages of ______.
First of all, ... Moreover, ... What's more, ...

On the other hand, we should not ignore its disadvantages.
To begin with, ... Besides, ... Finally, ...

【尾段 — 个人观点】
As far as I am concerned, ______ is more beneficial than harmful.
What we need to do is to make full use of its advantages while
avoiding its negative effects. Only in this way can we truly
benefit from it.
💡 高分替换词：important→significant/essential/vital | more and more→an increasing number of | I think→From my perspective
模板 2
建议/方案型
适用：书信建议、活动计划、改进方案
【首段 — 背景 + 目的】
I am writing to put forward some suggestions concerning ______.
As a ______, I have observed some issues that deserve our attention.

【中段 — 具体建议】
In the first place, I suggest that we should ______.
The reason is that ______.
In the second place, it would be beneficial if we could ______.
By doing so, we can ______.
Last but not least, ______ is also of great importance.

【尾段 — 期待与总结】
I sincerely hope that my suggestions will be taken into
consideration. With joint efforts, I am convinced that
______ can be solved effectively.
模板 3
重要性论述型
适用：健康、社交技能、大学语文、跨文化能力等
【首段 — 点明重要性】
It is universally acknowledged that ______ plays an
indispensable role in ______. Without it, we would
find it difficult to ______.

【中段 — 论证重要性】
There are several reasons to account for its importance.
First and foremost, ______. For example, ______.
Furthermore, ______. A case in point is ______.
Additionally, ______. Statistics show that ______.

【尾段 — 总结 + 呼吁】
To sum up, the significance of ______ can never be
overemphasized. It is high time that we attached great
importance to ______ and took concrete actions to ______.
模板 4
记叙 + 感受型
适用：校园事件回忆、家乡发展、讲座汇报
【首段 — 引出事件】
When it comes to ______, what comes to my mind
first is ______. It happened ______, and it left
a deep impression on me.

【中段 — 描述 + 感受】
At that time, ______. What impressed me most was ______.
I can still vividly remember that ______.
This experience has taught me a valuable lesson: ______.
Moreover, I came to realize that ______.

【尾段 — 总结意义】
Looking back, I feel ______. It not only ______
but also ______. I will always cherish this memory.
模板 5
现象评论型（万能备用）
适用：大部分开放式话题
【首段 — 现象描述】
Nowadays, there is a growing concern over ______.
This phenomenon has become increasingly common.

【中段 — 原因/影响分析】
Several factors contribute to this trend.
To begin with, ______. In addition, ______.
As a result, it has exerted profound effects on ______.
On the positive side, ______. However, it may
also lead to ______.

【尾段 — 个人看法】
From where I stand, ______. We should adopt a
rational attitude and take effective measures.

📝 五、六级通用写作模板（4 大类型）

模板 1
给定首句论证型（六级最常见，占 70%+）
适用：绝大多数六级题目 — 自律、目标、AI、社交媒体等
【给定首句】______.

【承接 — 展开论述】
This statement reminds us that ______. In today's
society, this issue deserves our serious consideration.

【中段一 — 观点 1 + 例证】
First and foremost, ______. To put it more specifically,
______. For instance, ______. This example clearly
illustrates that ______.

【中段二 — 观点 2 + 分析】
Furthermore, ______. A recent survey indicates that
______. If we fail to ______, we may ______.
Therefore, it is of great necessity that we ______.

【尾段 — 总结升华】
In conclusion, ______. Only when we ______ can we
______. The future belongs to those who ______.
💡 关键词替换：important→of paramount importance/indispensable | we should→it is imperative that we (should) | lead to→give rise to/contribute to
模板 2
图表作文型（近年仅 2021.06 考过，但需警惕回归）
适用：柱状图/折线图描述 + 原因分析 + 趋势预测
【首段 — 描述图表】
As is vividly demonstrated in the chart, ______ has
experienced a dramatic change over the past ______.
The figure increased/decreased from ______ to ______.

【中段 — 分析原因】
What accounts for this remarkable change? From my point
of view, there are at least three contributing factors.
To begin with, ______. Moreover, ______.
Last but not least, ______.

【尾段 — 预测/评论】
Based on the analysis above, we can safely predict that
this trend will continue in the foreseeable future.
💡 图表描述词：上升→increase/rise/climb/soar | 下降→decrease/decline/drop/plunge | 急剧地→dramatically/sharply | 逐渐地→gradually/steadily
模板 3
社会现象评论型
适用：追星、虚假信息、环保、助人为乐等
【首段 — 现象引入】
There is no denying that ______ has become a heated
topic in contemporary society. People's views on this
issue vary from person to person.

【中段 — 现象分析 + 我的看法】
Primarily, ______. In the second place, ______.
As for me, ______. On the one hand, ______.
On the other hand, ______.

【尾段 — 解决方案】
To address this issue, joint efforts are required.
The government should ______. Meanwhile, individuals
ought to ______. Only through collective actions
can we ______.
模板 4
个人素养/能力培养型
适用：终身学习、团队精神、批判思维、自律、目标设定
【首段 — 引入能力主题】
In the modern era, ______ has been regarded as one
of the most essential qualities for ______.

【中段 — 论证重要性】
From a personal perspective, ______. For instance, ______.
From a social perspective, ______. Studies show that ______.
How can we cultivate this quality?
First, ______. Second, ______. Third, ______.

【尾段 — 总结】
To sum up, ______ is not only a personal asset but
also a social need. It is high time we realized its
importance and took practical steps to develop it.

⚠️ 七、四六级写作常见陷阱与对策

  • 跑题审题 3 分钟，圈出关键词，确定文体和角度再动笔
  • 字数不够四级写满 150 词，六级写满 180 词。举例和解释能自然扩句
  • 语法错误不确定的复杂句宁可用简单句。主谓一致、时态一致必须检查
  • 模板痕迹重模板是骨架，具体内容用自己的话填充，避免整段生搬硬套
  • 没有例子每个论点配一个例子 — 可以是数据、故事、假设情景
  • 虎头蛇尾结尾至少写 2-3 句，不要一句话匆匆结束
  • 中式英语多背地道表达，避免逐字翻译。不确定时用简单结构
  • 卷面潦草字迹清晰 + 段落分明 + 少涂改，印象分很重要
