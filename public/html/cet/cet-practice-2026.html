CET6 #3李时珍与《本草纲目》
李时珍是明代著名医药学家，生于1518年，被后人尊称为"药圣"。他自幼随父学医，立志编撰一部全面系统的药学典籍，以纠正古代本草书中的诸多错误。为此，他不畏艰辛，走遍湖北、江西、广东等地，亲自采集药物标本，向农民、渔夫、猎人等各类人群虚心求教。历经二十七年潜心研究，他终于完成了这部划时代的巨著。《本草纲目》共五十二卷，收录药物近两千种，附插图一千一百幅，记录药方一万一千余个。该书出版后迅速流传至日本、朝鲜及欧洲各国，对世界医药学的发展产生了深远影响。
Li Shizhen was a renowned physician and pharmacologist of the Ming Dynasty, born in 1518. He has been revered by later generations as the "Saint of Medicine." He studied medicine under his father from childhood and resolved to compile a comprehensive and systematic pharmacological classic to correct numerous errors found in ancient herbal texts. To this end, undaunted by hardship, he traveled extensively through Hubei, Jiangxi, Guangdong, and other provinces, personally collecting medicinal specimens and humbly consulting farmers, fishermen, hunters, and people from all walks of life. After 27 years of dedicated research, he finally completed this epoch-making monumental work. The Compendium of Materia Medica consists of 52 volumes, covering nearly 2,000 medicinal substances, accompanied by 1,100 illustrations and recording more than 11,000 prescriptions. After its publication, the book rapidly spread to Japan, Korea, and European countries, exerting a profound influence on the development of world medicine.

CET6 #5古琴文化
古琴是中国最古老的拨弦乐器之一，历史超过三千年，在中国传统文化中具有极其崇高的地位。古琴形制古朴，通常有七根弦，音色深沉含蓄。在中国古代，古琴不仅是一件乐器，更是文人雅士修身养性、表达情志的重要工具。儒家将"琴棋书画"列为君子必修的四艺，古琴位居首位。中国古代许多名篇都与古琴有关，如"高山流水"描述了俞伯牙与钟子期之间惺惺相惜的知音之情。唐代诗人王维、宋代文学家苏轼都是古琴的忠实爱好者。2003年，古琴被联合国教科文组织列为人类非物质文化遗产。
The guqin is one of China's oldest plucked string instruments, with a history of over 3,000 years, and holds an extremely lofty status in traditional Chinese culture. The guqin has a simple and classical form, typically with seven strings, and is known for its deep and understated timbre. In ancient China, the guqin was not merely a musical instrument but an important means for scholars and intellectuals to cultivate their character and express their feelings. Confucianism listed the "four arts of the scholar"—qin (guqin), qi (chess), shu (calligraphy), and hua (painting)—as essential accomplishments of the cultured gentleman, with the guqin placed first. Many famous works in ancient Chinese literature are associated with the guqin, such as "High Mountains and Flowing Waters," which depicts the soulmate bond between Yu Boya and Zhong Ziqi. Both the Tang Dynasty poet Wang Wei and the Song Dynasty writer Su Shi were devoted enthusiasts of the guqin. In 2003, the guqin was inscribed by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

CET6 #6苏州园林
苏州园林是中国传统园林艺术的杰出代表，主要分布于江苏省苏州市，现存园林九十余处。苏州园林以其精巧的布局、丰富的意境和深厚的文化底蕴著称于世，被誉为"咫尺之内造乾坤"。苏州园林善用借景、框景等造园手法，通过巧妙设计，使有限的空间呈现出丰富的景观层次。拙政园是苏州最大的私家园林，始建于明朝，面积约为五万二千平方米，以水景见长，其亭台楼阁与山水花木巧妙搭配，步移景异。1997年，拙政园等九座苏州园林被列入世界文化遗产名录。
Suzhou gardens are an outstanding representative of traditional Chinese garden art, mainly distributed across Suzhou, Jiangsu Province, with more than 90 gardens surviving today. Suzhou gardens are world-renowned for their ingenious layout, rich artistic conception, and profound cultural heritage, earning the praise of "creating a universe within a tiny space." Suzhou gardens make skillful use of techniques such as borrowed scenery and framed views, presenting rich layers of landscape within a limited space through clever design. The Humble Administrator's Garden (Zhuozhengyuan) is the largest private garden in Suzhou. Built during the Ming Dynasty, it covers an area of approximately 52,000 square meters and is particularly noted for its water scenery, with pavilions, terraces, and towers artfully arranged alongside hills, water, and vegetation so that the view changes with every step. In 1997, nine Suzhou gardens, including the Humble Administrator's Garden, were inscribed on the World Cultural Heritage List.

CET6 #7人工智能与数字经济
人工智能是引领新一轮科技革命和产业变革的战略性技术，正深刻改变着人类的生产方式和生活方式。中国高度重视人工智能的发展，已将其列为国家发展战略的重要组成部分。近年来，中国在自然语言处理、计算机视觉、语音识别等领域取得了重大突破，涌现出一批具有全球影响力的AI企业。2025年初，大语言模型DeepSeek发布，以低成本、高性能的特点引发全球广泛关注，被视为中国AI领域的重要突破。与此同时，中国数字经济规模持续扩大，电商直播、跨境电商等新业态不断涌现。
Artificial intelligence (AI) is a strategic technology leading a new round of technological revolution and industrial transformation, profoundly changing the way people produce and live. China attaches great importance to the development of AI and has incorporated it as an important part of its national development strategy. In recent years, China has achieved major breakthroughs in AI fields such as natural language processing, computer vision, and speech recognition, giving rise to a number of AI enterprises with global influence. CET6 #8中国高铁
中国高速铁路经历了从引进消化吸收到自主创新的跨越式发展。2008年，中国第一条设计时速三百五十公里的高铁线路——京津城际铁路正式通车运营。短短十余年间，中国高铁运营里程从零起步，增长至超过四万公里，占全球高铁总里程约三分之二。中国自主研发的复兴号动车组，最高运营时速可达三百五十公里，代表了当今世界领先的高铁技术水平。高铁的飞速发展打破了地理隔阂，大大缩短了城市间的旅行时间，也有力推动了沿线地区的经济腾飞。
China's high-speed rail has undergone leap-forward development, evolving from the introduction and assimilation of foreign technology to independent innovation. In 2008, China's first high-speed rail line designed for a top speed of 350 kilometers per hour—the Beijing-Tianjin Intercity Railway—was officially put into operation. In just over a decade, China's operational high-speed rail mileage grew from zero to over 40,000 kilometers, accounting for approximately two-thirds of the world's total high-speed rail mileage. The Fuxing EMU, independently developed by China, can reach a maximum operating speed of 350 kilometers per hour and represents world-leading high-speed rail technology today. The rapid development of high-speed rail has broken down geographical barriers, greatly shortened travel time between cities, and has also powerfully promoted the economic takeoff of regions along the lines.

CET6 #9乡村振兴
乡村振兴是中国全面建设社会主义现代化国家的重大战略。近年来，随着乡村振兴政策的深入推进，中国农村发生了翻天覆地的变化。农村基础设施显著改善，道路、电网、通信网络已基本覆盖所有行政村。农村电商蓬勃发展，越来越多的农民通过网络直播平台销售农产品，收入大幅提高。特色农业、生态旅游等新业态在农村不断涌现，有力推动了农村产业融合发展。中国已兑现承诺，成为全球唯一在规定时限内彻底消除绝对贫困的大型发展中国家。
Rural revitalization is a major strategy in China's comprehensive effort to build a modern socialist country. In recent years, with the deepening implementation of rural revitalization policies, earth-shattering changes have taken place in China's rural areas. Rural infrastructure has improved significantly, with roads, power grids, and communication networks now basically covering all administrative villages. Rural e-commerce has flourished, with more and more farmers selling agricultural products via live-streaming platforms, leading to a substantial increase in income. New business forms such as specialty agriculture and eco-tourism have continuously emerged in rural areas, effectively driving the integrated development of rural industries. China has fulfilled its commitment, becoming the only large developing country in the world to have completely eliminated absolute poverty within the stipulated timeframe.
